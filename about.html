ANATOLIAN & EAST MEDITERRANEAN GOURMET
Welcome to a unique culinary journey extending from the sizzling kebabs of the East and the fragrant sherbets of the Levant, to the mist-shrouded mountains of the Black Sea and the olive-scented shores of the Aegean. Shaped by centuries of interwoven civilizations, dining here is far more than mere sustenance—it is a cherished conversation, a cultural legacy, and a timeless fountain of well-being passed down through generations.
Our Culinary Map

Heritage of the East & Southeast

Aromatic kebabs fresh from clay ovens, hand-kneaded artisan çiğ köfte from traditional Urfa gatherings, and golden, paper-thin layered baklava from Gaziantep.

Bounty of Central & Eastern Anatolia

Golden-baked savory kete from Sivas, roasted high-altitude goose from the snowy mountains of Ardahan, and centuries-old secrets of traditional pastry making.

Natural Traditions of the Black Sea

Nutrient-rich black kale nurtured by crisp mountain air, golden cornmeal delicacies, and wholesome, rustic mountain pasture cuisine.

Istanbul & Ottoman Palace Cuisine

Centuries-old royal recipes, elegant slow-cooked casseroles pairing tender meats with fruits and delicate spices, and refreshing herbal sherbets.

Wholesome Elegance of the Aegean & Mediterranean

Fresh coastal samphire, delicate tender zucchini flowers harvested at dawn, and nutrient-dense artichoke hearts offering unmatched freshness, flavor, and vitality found nowhere else in the world.
